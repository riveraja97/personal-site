About Me 🌸

Hello! My name is Jaelyn. I'm a recent college graduate with a Bachelor's degree and a passion for technology, creativity, testing, and design. I love exploring the intersection of human-centered design and technical problem-solving. I'm always eager to learn new skills and take on exciting challenges. I'm also committed to building meaningful digital user experiences.

I enjoy creating responsive websites, interactive browser games, and applications that connect users with useful technology. I'm especially interested in how design, research, and development work together.

I love learning new skills, collaborating with others, and turning challenges into opportunities to grow.
